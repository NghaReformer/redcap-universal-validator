Some values break a rule and are still right: a weight above the range the protocol expected for a patient who really weighs that much, or a visit date outside its window because the clinic was closed. A rule that holds the save then forces the person to enter a wrong value or leave the form. @UVJUSTIFY names a second field where the reason is written. Once that field holds a reason, the broken rule no longer holds the save, and the audit and the scan report the finding as "saved with a reason", apart from the open ones.

Syntax

@UVRANGE={"hard":[30,250]} @UVJUSTIFY={"reason":"[weight_reason]"}

@UVWINDOW={"from":"[visit_date_bl]","window":[21,35],"blockSave":"hard"} @UVJUSTIFY={"reason":"[visit_late_why]","message":"Say why the visit was outside its window."}
KeyMeaning
reasonThe field that holds the reason, written as one field reference such as "[weight_reason]". It must be on the same form as the field, and be a Text or Notes field, or a dropdown, radio or SQL field whose choices are the reasons you allow. It cannot be the field itself. The person must fill it in, so the module refuses the record ID field, a field REDCap fills in itself (@CALCTEXT, @CALCDATE, @DEFAULT, @SETVALUE, @PREFILL, @NOW, @TODAY and their -SERVER and -UTC forms, @USERNAME, @APPUSERNAME-APP, @LATITUDE, @LONGITUDE, @DOWNLOAD-COUNT), a field with any form of @READONLY, and a field that carries @UVJUSTIFY itself
messageThe note under the field that asks for the reason. It may be a map of language ids to texts, as for the other tags. Without it the note reads "Fix this value, or say why it is right in" the reason field's label

The tag takes no when, no caseSensitive, no blockSave and no event or instance references. A reason stands for the field's other rules whenever they apply, and each of those rules keeps its own when and blockSave.

Which rules a reason stands for

@UVALIDATE, @UVASSERT, @UVREQUIRED, @UVCHOICES, @UVWINDOW, @UVEXISTS, @UVRANGE and @UVDELTA on the same field. Never @UVUNIQUE: a reason does not make a duplicate identifier safe. Never @UVWRITEONCE: a locked field stays locked, and only the roles the tag names can change it. A @UVJUSTIFY on a field that none of these rules checks shows a configuration error under that field.

What counts as a reason

  • Any text left after spaces, tabs and line breaks are taken from both ends. A blank field gives no reason.
  • A missing data code gives no reason. A reason field marked "unknown" still asks for one.
  • On the form, a reason field the person cannot see gives no reason: its row hidden by branching logic, or, for a reason field embedded in another field's label, the row it sits in. The reason has to be visible where the value is entered.
  • A reason stands until someone clears it. A later, different broken value of the field saves under the same reason, so clear the reason field when the value changes for another reason.
  • The reason itself is never written to the log or the scan. Only the name of the reason field is.

On the form

  • While a rule a reason stands for holds the save and the reason field gives no reason, a note under the field asks for one, and the save message adds "or give a reason in" with the reason field's label.
  • Once the reason is given, the field no longer holds the save for those rules. Their own notes stay, so the person still sees what the rule found.
  • A reason never lets anything else through. A rule with blockSave "off" still only warns. A lookup still being checked (@UVUNIQUE, @UVEXISTS) still holds the save until its answer comes. A field locked by @UVWRITEONCE stays locked.
  • On a rule that only warns (blockSave "off", or an @UVDELTA that compares with another event or instance, which the page shows only as advice) a reason never shows a note on the page. It only labels the finding in the audit and the scan.
  • On a survey whose reason field is on another page, the tag does nothing on the page of the field. The audit after the save still reads the reason.

Audit and scan

The post-save audit logs a broken rule whose reason is given as uvalidate-justified instead of invalid-id-saved, with justified_by naming the reason field. The reason is read from the same record, event and instance as the value. The Validation scan counts these findings apart: the summary reads "and N saved with a reason", the findings table gets a "Saved with a reason" column, and a scan whose findings all have a reason counts no open violations. A reason field the audit could not read leaves the finding open.
